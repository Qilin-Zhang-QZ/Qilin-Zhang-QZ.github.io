About me

After earning his bachelor's and master's degrees in Library Science from Nanjing University, Qilin joined Southwest University as a librarian in 2015, working in his hometown of Chongqing and researching reading-promotion policies across different countries. Three years later, he decided to pursue a doctoral degree, broadening and deepening his research interests. Through a historical study of citation norms from a comparative China–West perspective, Qilin completed his PhD (2021), also at Nanjing University, and subsequently became a faculty member, being promoted to Associate Professor in 2023. In 2026, he began a one-year visiting research position at Amsterdam UMC in the Netherlands, focusing on questionable citation practices, and welcomes opportunities for collaboration — feel free to reach out.

To date, Qilin has published 30 first-authored journal articles (most in Chinese), and has also led several research projects, including one funded by China National Social Science Fund.
